# Daksh Kumawat — 3D Interactive Portfolio

> A professional, interactive portfolio built to showcase my journey as a first-year Computer Science student, my projects, technical skills, and early experience in software development.

## 🚀 About

This is my personal portfolio website, designed as more than a traditional resume-style webpage.

The portfolio presents my learning journey through an interactive 3D and scroll-driven experience while keeping the content focused on real projects, practical development, and continuous learning.

I am currently building my foundation in Computer Science through hands-on development with C, Python, Git, GitHub, and Linux, while exploring my future direction toward Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science.

## 🧠 Portfolio Philosophy

This portfolio is built around a simple idea:

**Don't just show what I know. Show what I build.**

Instead of presenting a long list of technologies, the website focuses on projects, practical experimentation, learning milestones, and evidence of continuous development.

The goal is to represent where I am today while showing the direction I am working toward.

## 🎨 Design Direction

The visual identity intentionally avoids the typical generic AI portfolio aesthetic.

Instead, the design focuses on:

- Dark professional interface
- Warm orange accent
- Strong typography
- Depth and perspective
- Controlled 3D effects
- Cinematic scrolling
- Minimal but meaningful motion
- Clear information hierarchy
- Editorial composition

The animations are designed to support the content rather than distract from it.

## 📱 Responsive Experience

The website is designed to work across:

- Desktop
- Laptop
- Tablet
- Mobile

3D and pointer interactions are progressively simplified on smaller devices to maintain usability and performance.

## ♿ Accessibility & Performance

The portfolio includes considerations for:

- Reduced-motion preferences
- Keyboard navigation
- Responsive layouts
- Progressive enhancement
- Lightweight vanilla JavaScript
- Native browser APIs
- Minimal external dependencies

The website is designed so that its core content remains accessible even when advanced JavaScript interactions are unavailable.
